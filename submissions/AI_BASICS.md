Factual technical notes, not invented personal reflection or a completed Devpost entry.

## Product and demonstration

ReEntry is a working local Chrome extension/web app for recovering interrupted work. The complete demo captures selected instructions, saves user-confirmed progress, returns after a fictional instruction change, shows real AI interpretation with exact evidence, corrects/restores a checkpoint and opens the reviewed work link.

Public source: https://github.com/kirmada1509/reentry. Reproducible setup: README and docs/RELEASE.md. Add the actual public YouTube/Vimeo recording URL before submitting.
